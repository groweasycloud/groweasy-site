FAQ

Common Questions

Is GrowEasy really free? +
Yes. The core feature set — invoicing, inventory, expenses, and FBR tax certificates — is completely free and always will be. Advanced features like multi-channel sync, AI insights, and unlimited team seats require a paid plan.
Is it really FBR compliant? +
Yes. GrowEasy generates tax certificates with NTN/STRN fields, auto QR codes, and the format your accountant needs for FBR filing. Sales tax (GST), withholding tax, and income tax certificate generation are all supported.
Can I import my existing Excel data? +
Yes. Download the CSV template, copy-paste from your Excel file, and upload. 1000+ products import in under 60 seconds with smart validation to catch errors before they enter your system.
Does it work on mobile? +
Yes. GrowEasy is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — it works on any phone browser, can be installed like a native app, and basic features work offline. Full business visibility on the go.
Where is my data stored? +
Your data is hosted in Pakistan. No tracking, no selling your business data. The UPC barcodes you generate via GrowEasy are yours forever and can be exported anytime.
Can my accountant access it? +
Yes. You can give your accountant or auditor a secure read-only access link. They can view reports, download tax certificates, and export data without being able to change anything.
